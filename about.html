About Harvest of Hygiene

We are a small NPO from Pretoria, Gauteng. We started in 2024 because we saw many girls missing school every month because they don't have pads. Our mission is simple - make sure every girl has sanitary products and knows about health education.

We work mostly in Soweto and Tembisa schools. We give free packs and we also teach girls about hygiene.

What do you put in the sanitary pack? ▼
We put Bodyform pads, Nivea deodorant, small soap, tissues and a booklet about periods. See photo on home page. Cost R50 per pack.
Where do you work? ▼
We work in Gauteng - Soweto, Tembisa and Pretoria schools. Our office is in Pretoria, distribution at 45 Vilakazi Street, Soweto.
How can I donate? ▼
Go to Get Involved page or contact us on WhatsApp 071 123 4567. We accept pads, soap, money. R50 = 1 pack.
